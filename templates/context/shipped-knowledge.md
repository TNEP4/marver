# Delivered - what was delivered, to whom, and when

The only file that says what is delivered. Every evidence cell carries its level, and `confirmed`
and `reported` carry a citation:

- `confirmed` - observed in a system of record: the client's written acceptance, the sent email,
  the signed-off document.
- `reported` - a written claim, cited: a meeting note, a changelog line.
- `unknown` - neither, with the reason. An honest unknown is the right answer.
