# Marver for Mac

The Mac app for [Marver](https://github.com/TNEP4/marver), the design canvas that lives in your repo: every canvas on
your Mac in one window, a tab each, with notices when a canvas needs you.

This repository holds the app's releases and nothing else. The source is in
[`app/`](https://github.com/TNEP4/marver/tree/main/app) of the Marver repository, and issues go
[there too](https://github.com/TNEP4/marver/issues).

## Download

**[Marver-arm64.dmg](https://github.com/TNEP4/marver-app/releases/latest/download/Marver-arm64.dmg)** - the latest
release, signed and notarised by Apple.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 13 or later. Open the DMG and drag Marver to Applications. Marver keeps
itself up to date from this repository's releases.
