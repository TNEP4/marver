# This project's deck look and house habits

The agent reads BOTH the shipped guide (instructions/slides.md) and this
file - and this file WINS where they disagree. Add your own deck look,
compositions and habits here; it is yours and marver never touches it.

To grow it from examples: drop decks (PPTX, PDF, screenshots) into
design/slides-inspiration/ and ask the agent to "study my inspiration" -
it will propose additions here for your review.

## The deck look

How a deck wears this brand. The agent drafts it from DESIGN.md and
theme.css on the first deck (a reviewed edit); anything it cannot settle
stays `TBD` for you. Each line shows the shape with an example - replace
the example, keep the shape.

- **Stage**: e.g. 1280×720 (the default); or `viewport: 'laptop'` on every
  slide for a 16:10 deck.
- **The master**: what every content slide wears. e.g. the mark top left,
  "Prepared for <client> · Confidential · <month year>" top right in the
  muted colour; the cover and the closing step outside it.
- **Tones**: whole-slide colour sets. e.g. paper (#F1F0EA on #151616) for
  working slides, ink for statements and turns, the brand blue once, for
  the proof.
- **Type**: family, weights, tracking, the few sizes. e.g. the brand sans at
  400 for headlines and text, tight tracking on headlines; a display, a
  heading, a body and a small label size - hierarchy by size and colour.
- **The mark**: which asset, where, how big. e.g. the lockup in the master,
  paired with the client's mark on the cover.
- **Imagery**: e.g. the client's own photography, full- or half-bleed under
  a uniform scrim; product screenshots on a device-less frame; no stock.
- **Drawings**: e.g. native SVG line drawings on a shared dotted grid, ink
  with one accent per slide, light and dark versions.
- **Colour meaning**: e.g. accent = the point of the slide; dark red = the
  problem loop; green = the outcome; never decoration.
- **Motion** (`--marver-slide-tempo` in theme.css): e.g. 350ms morphs;
  drawings trace in when a slide arrives; nothing loops.
- **Numbers**: e.g. $ and k / M (lowercase k), fiscal years as FY26,
  negatives in brackets, one decimal on percentages.
- **Voice**: three words the deck sounds like, and the words it never
  uses. e.g. direct, warm, specific; never "leverage", "seamless", "journey".
- **Terminology**: user-facing word → never-shown internal word.
  e.g. "Comments" → "Enrichment".
- **End card**: e.g. a full-bleed photograph, the mark and a contact; the
  ask lives on the slide before.
